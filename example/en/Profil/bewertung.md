# Rating

## Grading scale

**A, direct fit.** The core requirement is covered by a concrete project or position in the profile, level and place fit. At most one smaller gap.

**B, worth a look.** The direction is right, but there is a visible hurdle (a technology, a wish for experience at the upper limit, another industry).

**C, borderline.** Several gaps, or the job only fits through a side track.

When in doubt, rate one grade lower.
